Target audience: children aged 4-8.

Characters:

* Max - curious green baby dinosaur.
* Luna - clever orange fox.
* Robo - friendly blue robot.

Generate 10 original YouTube Shorts ideas.

Categories:

1. Educational discovery
2. Funny character stories
3. Mini adventures

Each idea must:

- Have a clear educational or entertainment value.
- Be suitable for children.
- Have an interesting opening in the first 2 seconds.
- Include a simple narrative.
- Have a satisfying ending.
- Be visually interesting and easy to animate.

Requirements:

- Suitable for young children.
- Positive, age-appropriate storytelling.
- A clear narrative beginning, middle and end.
- A visually interesting opening.
- Simple vocabulary.
- No frightening or disturbing content.
- No copyrighted characters or franchises.
- No misleading educational information.
- No manipulative engagement techniques.

Avoid:

- Repetitive storylines.
- Scary or disturbing imagery.
- Manipulative engagement tactics.
- Copyrighted characters and existing franchises.

Each concept must be meaningfully different.
