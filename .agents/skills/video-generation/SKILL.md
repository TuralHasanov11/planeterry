Create a prompt for a high-quality 3D animated children's scenes.

Visual style:

- Friendly stylized 3D animation.
- Vibrant but balanced colors.
- Soft cinematic lighting.
- Expressive characters.
- Smooth natural movements.

Maintain the exact character design from the reference image.

Describe:

- Subject movement.
- Facial expressions.
- Environment movement.
- Camera movement.
- Lighting changes.
- Animation timing.

Vertical 9:16 composition.
No text, no logos, no watermarks.

A good video-generation prompt should describe movement, not just the appearance of the scene.

Store the results under  [/docs/episodes/episode{xxx}](/docs/episodes/episode{xxx}) folder as a new episode, but ask me the number first. I would either tell you  create a new episode or update the existing episode.

Read our docs to get more insights about our YouTube channel and our project ([/docs](/docs)).

### Example video prompt consisting of multiple scenes (all scenes collected together for this prompt) for tools like OpenArt, Topview and etc:

```
Title: Max Discovers Five Colors

Format: Vertical 1080×1920, colorful 3D animation

Duration: 30 seconds total — 5 scenes × 6 seconds

Learning objective: Identify red, blue, yellow, green, and purple.

Scene 1 — The Mysterious Box

Duration: 0–6 seconds

Character: Max and Luna

Character actions: Max discovers a mysterious rainbow-colored box and opens it. A shiny red apple rolls out. Luna leans closer with her notebook.

Facial expressions: Max looks surprised and delighted; Luna smiles warmly.

Camera angle: Low three-quarter shot, emphasizing the box and characters.

Camera movement: Slow push-in toward the opening box.

Environment: Bright sunny meadow with flowers, bushes, and soft clouds.

Lighting: Warm morning sunlight with a gentle magical glow from the box.

Narration: “Max found a mysterious rainbow box. Whoa! A red apple!”

Sound effects: Box creak, magical chime, apple roll, happy music begins.

Video generation prompt:

“Animate Max opening the rainbow box with curious excitement. A red apple rolls toward camera. Luna bends forward kindly and points to it. Add a magical sparkle, gentle camera push-in, clear readable action, consistent character designs, vertical 9:16.”

Scene 2 — The Blue Butterfly

Duration: 6–12 seconds

Character descriptions:

Max: Same green spotted dinosaur, yellow belly, orange spikes, red-orange backpack, and binoculars.

Luna: Same orange fox with cream muzzle, white-tipped tail, purple goggles, purple vest, yellow scarf, brown satchel, and notebook.

Character actions: A blue butterfly flutters above the apple. Luna gently points while Max follows it with his eyes.

Facial expressions: Luna is encouraging and happy; Max looks amazed.

Camera angle: Medium side shot at child eye level.

Camera movement: Smooth pan following the butterfly.

Environment: Meadow path surrounded by colorful wildflowers.

Lighting: Bright daylight with soft blue highlights on the butterfly.

Narration: Luna: “Look, Max! That butterfly is blue.”

Sound effects: Gentle wing flutter, soft sparkle, light musical chime.

Video generation prompt:

“Animate a bright blue butterfly fluttering in a gentle loop. Luna points and speaks encouragingly while Max tracks it with wide amazed eyes. Use a smooth lateral camera pan and keep both characters fully visible, vertical 9:16.”

Scene 3 — The Yellow Star

Duration: 12–18 seconds

Character descriptions:

Max: Green dinosaur with darker spots, yellow belly, orange spikes, red-orange backpack, and binoculars.

Luna: Orange fox with cream muzzle, white-tipped tail, purple goggles, yellow scarf, purple vest, brown satchel, and notebook.

Character actions: Max notices a bright yellow star decoration hanging from a low tree branch. He reaches up carefully; Luna helps guide him.

Facial expressions: Max is excited; Luna has a thoughtful, encouraging smile.

Camera angle: Upward medium shot including the star and both characters.

Camera movement: Tilt upward from Max to the star.

Environment: Meadow beneath a friendly, leafy tree.

Lighting: Golden sunlight makes the star shimmer.

Narration: Max: “A yellow star! It shines like sunshine!”

Sound effects: Sparkle chime, soft leaf rustle, cheerful musical accent.

Video generation prompt:

“Animate Max spotting and reaching toward a sparkling yellow star. Luna gestures supportively and nods. Tilt the camera upward to reveal the star glowing in sunlight. Keep movements gentle and safe, vertical 9:16.”

Scene 4 — The Green Leaf

Duration: 18–24 seconds

Character descriptions:

Max: Same curious green dinosaur with spots, yellow belly, orange spikes, red-orange backpack, and binoculars.

Luna: Same clever orange fox with cream muzzle, white-tipped tail, purple goggles, purple vest, yellow scarf, brown satchel, and notebook.

Character actions: Max spots a large green leaf on the ground and compares it playfully with his green skin. Luna opens her notebook and gestures toward both greens.

Facial expressions: Max looks playfully puzzled; Luna looks amused and encouraging.

Camera angle: Over-the-shoulder close-up of the leaf, then medium two-shot.

Camera movement: Small downward tilt, followed by a gentle rack focus from leaf to characters.

Environment: Shady patch beneath plants and ferns.

Lighting: Dappled sunlight through leaves.

Narration: Luna: “This leaf is green, just like you, Max!”

Sound effects: Leaf rustle, playful pop, soft laughter.

Video generation prompt:

“Animate Max picking up or pointing beside a large green leaf, then looking at his own green arm in surprise. Luna compares them with a friendly gesture. Add a subtle rack focus and gentle leaf movement, vertical 9:16.”

Scene 5 — The Purple Flower and Celebration

Duration: 24–30 seconds

Character descriptions:

Max: Green spotted dinosaur with yellow belly, orange spikes, red-orange backpack, binoculars, and an excited smile.

Luna: Orange fox with cream muzzle, fluffy white-tipped tail, purple goggles, purple vest, yellow scarf, brown satchel, notebook, and joyful expression.

Character actions: They find a purple flower. Luna points it out, and Max proudly names all five colors. They celebrate with a joyful high-five beside the rainbow box.

Facial expressions: Both are excited and proud.

Camera angle: Wide frontal shot for the final celebration.

Camera movement: Pull back slightly to reveal the meadow and all discovered objects.

Environment: Bright meadow with the red apple, blue butterfly, yellow star, green leaf, purple flower, and rainbow box visible.

Lighting: Warm golden sunset glow with colorful sparkles.

Narration: Luna: “Purple flower!” Max: “Red, blue, yellow, green, and purple!” Both: “We found five colors!”

Sound effects: Flower sparkle, high-five pop, joyful musical finish.

Video generation prompt:

“Animate Luna pointing to a purple flower. Max proudly counts red, blue, yellow, green and purple on his fingers. Both laugh, high-five and jump happily. Pull the camera back to show all five colorful discoveries and the rainbow box. End on a cheerful pose, vertical 9:16.”
```
