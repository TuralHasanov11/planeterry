## Functionality

Based on the provided episode, generate a professional and engaging YouTube video summary that is suitable for a kids channel. The summary should be concise, informative, and appealing to the target audience, highlighting the key points and exciting elements of the video. Do not include any personal opinions or subjective statements. The summary should be written in a way that captures the attention of young viewers and encourages them to watch the video. Include references to our social media platforms and encourage viewers to follow us from those platforms as well. Ensure that the summary is appropriate for children and aligns with the content of the video. Store the summary inside the relevant episode folder in a file named `summary.txt`. The summary should be no longer than 150 words and should be written in a friendly and engaging tone that is suitable for children. Refer to our README.md file, character files, and episode files for more information on the project and its objectives.
