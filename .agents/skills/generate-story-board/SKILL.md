Format:

- Length: 30 seconds

Provide a complete, fun, professional, enticing storyboard that I can use immediately.

Refer to our [character bible files](..\..\..\docs\characters) and [world building images](..\..\..\docs\world-building) for more insights.

Generate the storyboard both as markdown file and collection of images representing the scenes (1 image per scene).

Example storyboard text:

```
## Panel 1 — Max Makes a Request

Time: 0:00–0:05  
Shot: Medium-wide establishing shot at the characters' eye level.

Visual composition: The small flower is clearly visible in the right foreground. Max stands to its left, turned toward Robo. Robo stands near Max, facing him and listening. Show enough of the garden to establish a sunny, inviting outdoor setting.

Action and expression: Max gestures toward the flower with an open, friendly expression. Robo turns his head toward Max attentively; his cyan eyes are bright and ready to help.

Camera: Gentle push-in toward Max and Robo, keeping the flower visible in the composition.

Dialogue
- Max: "Robo, could you give this flower a little water?"

Sound: Soft garden ambience and a light, cheerful musical bed.

Transition: Cut as Robo turns toward the watering can.

## Panel 2 — Robo Gets to Work

Time: 0:05–0:10  
Shot: Medium shot focused on Robo and the watering can.

Visual composition: Robo is centered, holding or reaching for the blue watering can. The flower remains visible in the background on the right, providing a visual clue to the correct target.

Action and expression: Robo picks up the can carefully. His face becomes serious and analytical, as though he is scanning for a target. A small head tilt and subtle antenna movement reinforce his concentration.

Camera: Slight push-in toward Robo's digital face, then a small tilt down to the watering can.

Dialogue
- Robo: "Affirmative! Locating thirsty target..."

Sound: Gentle robotic scanning beeps followed by a small confirmation beep.

## Panel 3 — The Wrong Target

Time: 0:10–0:15  
Shot: Medium shot, wide enough to show Robo, the watering can, and the still-dry flower.

Visual composition: Robo confidently tips the blue watering can toward himself. The flower remains visible on the right, untouched and dry. Keep the stream of water clearly readable so the joke is understood immediately.

Action and expression: Water pours harmlessly over Robo's helmet and down his body. He holds his confident pose for a brief beat, unaware of the mistake. His expression changes from focused confidence to a small, puzzled look as the water continues.

Camera: Hold steady for the reveal, with a tiny comedic pause after the first splash.

Dialogue
- Robo: "Target found. Beginning hydration!"

Sound: A short pouring sound, a playful splash, and a tiny robotic confirmation beep.

Transition: Cut to Max and Luna reacting.

## Panel 4 — A Helpful Correction

Time: 0:15–0:20  
Shot: Medium two-shot of Max and Luna, with Robo still visible at the edge or in the background.

Visual composition: Max is on the left, pointing toward the unexpectedly wet Robo. Luna stands beside him and points clearly toward the dry flower on the right. The audience should be able to see both the mistake and the correct target.

Action and expression: Max reacts with surprised, wide eyes but remains friendly. Luna is calm and encouraging, guiding Robo's attention toward the flower rather than laughing at him.

Camera: Brief reaction shot, then a subtle pan in the direction Luna points.

Dialogue
- Max: "Robo! You're watering the wrong thing!"
- Luna: "The flower is over there!"

Sound: A light comedic reaction accent, followed by a soft musical pause that leaves room for the correction.

Transition: Cut back to Robo looking down at himself.

## Panel 5 — Robo Understands

Time: 0:20–0:25  
Shot: Medium close-up of Robo, with Max beside him.

Visual composition: Robo's wet helmet and upper body are clearly visible. Max stands close enough to gesture toward the flower, which remains in the same screen position as before.

Action and expression: Robo looks down at his wet body. One cyan eye becomes a spiral while the other stays round; his head tilts in confusion. Max demonstrates the correct direction by gently guiding the watering can's spout toward the flower.

Camera: Hold on Robo's funny digital expression, then shift focus toward the watering can's new direction.

Dialogue
- Robo: "Oh! My sensors selected... me?"
- Max: "Beep boop! Try the spout this way!"

Sound: A short descending electronic wobble for Robo's realization, followed by a positive confirmation beep.

Transition: Follow the watering can's spout toward the flower.

## Panel 6 — Mission Accomplished

Time: 0:25–0:30  
Shot: Medium-wide group shot that echoes the opening composition.

Visual composition: The flower is again clearly visible on the right. Robo now faces it and holds the watering can in the correct direction. Max and Luna stand nearby, watching supportively.

Action and expression: Robo gently waters the flower. Max smiles and gives an approving reaction. Luna smiles warmly. Robo's digital face changes to a pleased, cheerful expression as he realizes he has completed the task correctly.

Camera: Gentle pull-back to include all three friends and the flower, ending on a clean, upbeat group composition.

Dialogue
- Robo: "System update: flowers need water too!"
- Luna: "A little for the flower, please!"
- Max: "Much better!"

Sound: Gentle water pour, a small happy chime, and a cheerful musical finish.

Ending: Hold the final happy image briefly so the success lands clearly before the video ends.
```
