Action & expression: Max gestures toward the flower with an open, friendly expression. Robo turns his head toward Max attentively; his cyan eyes are bright and ready to help. Camera: Medium-wide establishing shot at character eye level. Gentle push-in toward Max and Robo, keeping the flower visible. Dialogue: Max: “Robo, could you give this flower a little water?” Sound effects: Soft garden ambience and a light, cheerful musical bed. Scene 2 - Robo Gets to Work. Scene: Robo picks up a blue watering can. He studies the area with a serious, focused expression, as if scanning for the correct target. Action & expression: Robo picks up the can carefully. His face becomes serious and analytical, as though he is scanning for a target. A small head tilt and subtle antenna movement reinforce his concentration. Camera: Medium shot focused on Robo and the watering can. Slight push-in toward Robo’s digital face, then a small tilt down to the can. Dialogue: Robo: “Affirmative! Locating thirsty target...” Sound effects: Gentle robotic scanning beeps followed by a small confirmation beep. Scene 3 - The Wrong Target. Scene: Robo confidently tilts the watering can in the wrong direction and pours water over his own head and body. The flower remains completely dry. Water splashes harmlessly over Robo. Action & expression: Water pours harmlessly over Robo’s helmet and down his body. He holds his confident pose for a brief beat, unaware of the mistake. His expression changes from focused confidence to a small, puzzled look as the water continues. Camera: Medium shot wide enough to show Robo, the watering can, and the still-dry flower. Hold steady for the reveal, with a tiny comedic pause after the first splash. Dialogue: Robo: “Target found. Beginning hydration!” Sound effects: A short pouring sound, a playful splash, and a tiny robotic confirmation beep. Scene 4 — A Helpful Correction. Scene: Max points toward Robo in surprise. Luna calmly points toward the flower, helping Robo understand where the water should go. Action & expression: Max reacts with surprised, wide eyes but remains friendly. Luna is calm and encouraging, guiding Robo’s attention toward the flower rather than laughing at him. Camera: Medium two-shot of Max and Luna, with Robo visible at the edge or in the background. Brief reaction shot, then a subtle pan in the direction Luna points. Dialogue: Max: “Robo! You’re watering the wrong thing!” Luna: “The flower is over there!” Sound effects: A light comedic reaction accent, followed by a soft musical pause that leaves room for the correction. Scene 6 — Mission Accomplished. Scene: Robo turns the watering can toward the flower and waters it correctly. The flower gets a gentle splash. Max and Luna smile and celebrate with him. Robo looks pleased with his successful correction. Action & expression: Robo gently waters the flower. Max smiles and gives an approving reaction. Luna smiles warmly. Robo’s digital face changes to a pleased, cheerful expression as he realizes he has completed the task correctly. Camera: Medium-wide group shot echoing the opening composition. Gentle pull-back to include all three friends and the flower, ending on a clean, upbeat group composition. Dialogue: Robo: “System update: flowers need water too!” Luna: “A little for the flower, please!” Max: “Much better!” Sound effects: Gentle water pour, a small happy chime, and a cheerful musical finish.
