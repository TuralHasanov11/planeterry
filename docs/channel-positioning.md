# Channel Positioning --- Planeterry

## Channel Promise

For children aged 4--8 and parents watching with them, who want safe,
enjoyable entertainment.

## Creator Backstory

### Where I Was Before

I am a software developer striving to become a software architect in the
future.

I became interested in AI-powered content because I wanted to keep up
with the newest AI trends and explore ways to make money from them.

I noticed that many people, including people without technical
backgrounds, were generating good content and reportedly making
significant amounts of money from it. Seeing people advertise how they
were achieving this made me reflect on passive income and on the
importance of keeping my AI knowledge current. It also left me feeling
frustrated about the opportunity I might be missing.

### What Changed

After seeing many people create and promote AI-powered content channels
and report significant earnings, I decided to try it myself. A couple of
weeks before sharing this account, I created a channel and started
posting content.

### Where I Am Now

I have created three characters---Max, Luna, and Robo---with character
bibles and images. I have also prepared Planeterry's world-building
materials and images.

I have written GitHub Copilot skills to help generate ideas, stories,
scenes, and video prompts for the AI platforms I use, including OpenArt,
Topview, and KlingAI. I have stored these resources in my [Planeterry
GitHub repository](https://github.com/TuralHasanov11/planeterry).

I have generated and published the first video. At the time of this
interview, it had 29 views and 3 likes.

I am learning how to use AI video platforms effectively, including
prompt engineering, character design, and generating scenes and
storyboards with multiple scenes. I have also noticed that producing one
video can use a large number of OpenArt credits. Alongside production, I
am learning how to structure the business properly.

## Who Planeterry Helps Best

Planeterry is intended for children aged 4--8, without narrowing the
audience to a particular preference, personality, or interest. The
intention is for it to be a sweet, safe space that children can enjoy
regardless of their preferences. Parents can join them in watching.

The creator's own starting point is that of a software developer
interested in AI, learning new tools and workflows, and exploring
AI-powered content production. That backstory explains the creator's
route into building Planeterry; it does not mean the channel is aimed at
software developers.

## The Audience's Main Problems Planeterry Addresses

The creator identified two main issues:

1.  **Safety and trust.** Children's content should not contain
    irrelevant material, political or cultural messages, or content
    intended to manipulate children's thoughts. It should be appropriate
    for children and avoid violence and frightening material.
2.  **Respect for children's intelligence.** Some children's content can
    feel overly rudimentary or as if it is made for children who cannot
    think for themselves. Planeterry should let children use their
    brains and offer logical stories and thoughtful insights.

A third audience problem has not been identified.

## The Result Viewers Want

The intended result is an enjoyable entertainment experience---what the
creator described with the German word **"Unterhaltung."**

Learning may happen naturally as part of the entertainment. For example,
in the first video, "Max Discovers Five Colors," children can learn the
names of five colors. However, the channel is not positioned as formal
education or as a promise of specific learning outcomes.

## What Planeterry Offers

For now, Planeterry offers free children's entertainment on YouTube
through: - Animated Shorts. - Children's stories. - Longer videos that
combine shorter pieces into collections, such as a season containing
several episodes.

There are no other products or services identified at this stage.

## Who Planeterry Is Not For

Planeterry is not intended for viewers seeking: - Violent, frightening,
or otherwise inappropriate children's entertainment. - Content that
introduces irrelevant political or cultural messages. - Content designed
to manipulate children's thoughts. - Overly rudimentary entertainment
that does not respect children's ability to think.

Planeterry is not positioned primarily as an academic or formal
educational channel. Its main purpose is entertainment, with
opportunities for children to pick up things such as new words along the
way.

## Channel Context

The creator reports that the channel is available on YouTube Kids. This
is recorded as channel context, not as a guarantee that every video is
automatically safe or suitable.
