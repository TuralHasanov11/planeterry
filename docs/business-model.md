# Business Model

1. Model A — Original animated characters: Create your own recurring characters, such as a curious fox, a little robot, and a friendly dinosaur. They go on adventures, solve problems, and teach children something.
2. Model B — Educational Shorts: Numbers, colors, animals, basic science, vocabulary, geography, and simple puzzles. Easier to produce systematically; strong potential for evergreen content.
3. Model C — Funny animated stories: Short comedy, unexpected situations, visual jokes, and funny animal interactions.

We combine A + B + C. Building one fictional universe with original characters. Each character has a personality, and the characters participate in different formats:
- Educational: The little robot discovers five different planets.
- Funny: The dinosaur tries to eat a watermelon that's bigger than him.
- Storytelling: The fox helps a lost baby bird find its family.
- Interactive: Can you find the three hidden stars?

We initially target children aged 4–8, with parents as the secondary audience.
