# Monetization

## Other monetization opportunities

| Revenue source                            | Potential                                                |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| YouTube Shorts advertising                | Main initial target                                      |
| YouTube long-form videos                  | Important future revenue stream                          |
| Licensing original characters             | Long-term opportunity                                    |
| Children's books and printable activities | Potential additional products                            |
| Merchandise                               | Only after establishing recognizable characters          |
| Sponsorships                              | Possible, but children's advertising requires extra care |

Eventually, turn successful Shorts characters into longer 3–8 minute episodes, compilations, and educational stories.


## Instagram and TikTok

Use both as distribution channels, but don't assume that one million views on each platform generates the same revenue.

- Instagram Reels: brand discovery and potential partnerships.
- TikTok: discovery and testing short-form creative concepts.
- YouTube: primary destination for building a long-term children's media brand.
  TikTok's Creator Rewards Program, for example, requires original, high-quality videos longer than one minute for eligible rewards. Therefore, 15–30 second videos should not be planned around that revenue program.
