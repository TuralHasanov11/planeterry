# The AI production toolkit

## AI content production pipeline

1. Idea and trend research. Find concepts, hooks, and story ideas. ChatGPT · YouTube Analytics · Google Trends
2. Script and storyboard. Write original scripts and scene descriptions. ChatGPT · Claude
3. Character and scene generation. Create consistent characters and environments. Image generation · Reference images
4. Animation and video. Generate motion, expressions, and camera movements. Kling · Veo · Runway · Everygen
5. Audio and editing. Narration, music, sound effects, subtitles. ElevenLabs · CapCut · FFmpeg
6. Publishing and analytics. Schedule, distribute, measure, and improve. YouTube Studio · Meta Business Suite · TikTok Studio

## AI video generation platforms

### Openart

### Kling AI

Particularly worth testing for image-to-video animation and character movement.

### Google Veo

Worth testing for cinematic animation, scene generation, and more sophisticated motion.

### Runway

Good candidate for a more professional creative workflow, including generation and editing.

## Character and image generation

### Midjourney

Excellent candidate for developing a consistent visual identity and polished character concepts.

### Google Nano Banana

Useful for reference-based image generation and character variations.

### ComfyUI

Best technical opportunity. Node-based workflows, reusable generation pipelines, and local model integration. Particularly interesting if you eventually want more control and lower marginal costs.

## Voice generation

### ElevenLabs

Expressive narration and character voices.

## Editing and sound

### CapCut

Quick editing, captions, transitions, and sound effects.

### FFmpeg

Essential for automation. Automatically combine clips, narration, music, subtitles, and export videos.
