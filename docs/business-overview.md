# Business Overview --- Planeterry

## Business

Planeterry is an AI-powered children's content production business and a
YouTube children's entertainment channel.

## Audience

-   Primary audience: children aged 4--8.
-   Parents may also be part of the audience.

## Content and Offerings

Planeterry creates: - Animated short videos. - Children's stories. -
Longer videos that combine shorter pieces into collections, such as a
season containing several episodes.

## Content Boundaries

The content should be appropriate for children. It should not be: -
Violent. - Frightening. - Inappropriate children's entertainment.

No specific age group has been defined as excluded from the audience.

## Voice and Communication Style

The preferred style is: - Playful, funny, and energetic. - Friendly,
warm, and encouraging. - Curious and adventurous. - Educational, but
never boring. - Written and spoken in simple language children can
easily understand.

## Current YouTube Performance

The first YouTube Short posted is titled **"Max Discovers Five
Colors"**.

At the time this overview was prepared, it had: - 18 views. - 5 likes.

The creator feels the content needs a boost because 18 views is a small
number.

No other video performance, email results, or successful content formats
have been identified so far.

## YouTube and Business Goals

The channel is intended to: 1. Build a recognizable children's
entertainment brand. 2. Grow subscribers and views. 3. Generate
advertising revenue and eventually passive income. 4. Create a
sustainable business around AI-powered content production.

## Revenue Model

-   Current plan: generate income through YouTube advertising.
-   Additional income methods may be considered in the future, but none
    have been decided on yet.
