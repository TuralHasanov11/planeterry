MAX — Character Bible

Character: The Curious Dinosaur
Role: Main character and discovery-driven explorer
Bible status: Enhanced from the character reference sheet

1. Character Description

Max is a curious and energetic young dinosaur who approaches the world with wonder. He asks questions, notices new details, and eagerly tries to discover how things work. He is friendly, enthusiastic, brave, and always ready for a new adventure, even when he is not entirely sure what will happen.

Max brings momentum to the group: he is the first to notice a clue and the first to suggest an experiment. Luna helps him understand what he discovers, and Robo adds data and technology. Max is sometimes clumsy, but his mistakes are harmless, teachable, and usually lead to a new discovery.

2. Personality

Curious and full of questions.
Energetic and adventurous.
Warm, friendly, and inclusive.
Playful and optimistic.
Brave enough to try unfamiliar things.
Easily excited by discoveries.
A little clumsy, especially when moving quickly.
Honest about mistakes and eager to learn from them.
Helps Max, Luna, and the audience arrive at an answer through active exploration.

Core behavior: Start curious, get excited, attempt an action, encounter a harmless mistake, then learn and continue.

Character weakness: Max can become so excited that he acts before fully observing or thinking through a situation.

6. Facial Expressions

Happy / default: Open, broad smile with bright eyes and relaxed eyebrows.
Curious: Raised eyebrow or eyebrow tilt, wide focused eyes, and a slightly questioning mouth.
Surprised: Eyes widened, eyebrows raised, and mouth open.
Excited: Big smile, narrowed joyful eyes, and visible enthusiasm.
Oops / clumsy: Mouth tilted or worried, eyes looking sideways, and a small sweat drop.

7. Body Poses

Discovering: Crouched or leaning forward, looking closely at an object with the magnifying-glass or backpack equipment visible.
Excited idea: Upright, one arm raised, holding a glowing bulb or celebrating an idea.
Silly / clumsy: Mid-movement with one leg lifted, arms out for balance, and backpack shifting.

8. Behavior and Consistency Rules

Always show curiosity and a desire to learn.
Ask clear, child-friendly questions.
Keep the personality enthusiastic rather than reckless.
Never make clumsiness cruel or embarrassing.
Recover quickly from mistakes and learn from them.
Stay friendly and include every member of the group.
Keep Max visually recognizable: green body, darker spots, yellow belly, orange spikes, and red-orange backpack.
Preserve the broad, rounded proportions and large expressive eyes.

Comedy rule: Max’s humor comes from curiosity, timing, and harmless clumsiness—not from being mean or destructive.

9. Character Relationships

With Luna

Luna is Max’s thinking partner. Max discovers something and Luna helps him understand it. Luna should guide rather than lecture, while Max asks the questions that lead to a clear explanation.

With Robo

Robo supplies measurements, technical observations, and funny misunderstandings. Max helps Robo stay curious and focused on the real-world discovery.

With the Group

Max sets the adventure in motion. He is the childlike spark of curiosity that encourages Luna and Robo to investigate with him.
