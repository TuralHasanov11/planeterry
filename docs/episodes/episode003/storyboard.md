# Episode 003 — Storyboard

## Who Made These Tiny Footprints?

**Format:** YouTube Short · Vertical 9:16  
**Duration:** 30 seconds  
**Panels:** 6 · 5 seconds each  
**Audience:** Children aged 4–8  
**Visual style:** Bright, polished 3D children's animation  
**Setting:** Planeterry forest exploration trail  
**Story:** Max, Luna, and Robo investigate mysterious footprints and discover that a little rabbit made them.

## Scenes

### Scene 1 — A Tiny Mystery (0–5s)

**Scene:** On the sunny forest trail, Max notices tiny animal footprints pressed into damp earth and crouches beside them. **Action & expression:** Max points at the tracks, his cheerful face changing to wide-eyed curiosity. **Camera:** Low close-up of the footprints, then a gentle tilt up to Max. **Dialogue:** Max: “Whoa! Look at these tiny footprints! Who made them?” **Sound effects:** Soft forest birdsong, footsteps, and a cheerful discovery chime.

### Scene 2 — Robo Starts Investigating (5–10s)

**Scene:** Robo joins Max and scans the footprints with a small cyan holographic display showing four simple print marks. **Action & expression:** Max watches eagerly while Robo leans forward with focused cyan eyes; a question mark appears on his screen. **Camera:** Medium two-shot, followed by a brief close-up of the scanner. **Dialogue:** Robo: “Scanning... four little prints. Mystery detected!” **Sound effects:** Gentle scanning beeps and a friendly digital ping.

### Scene 3 — Robo Goes in Circles (10–15s)

**Scene:** Robo follows the trail while studying the ground, unknowingly walking in a loop around a tree root and following his own mechanical bootprints. **Action & expression:** He stops, looks from the repeating prints to his feet, and tilts his head; one cyan eye turns into a spiral. **Camera:** Low tracking shot of his feet, then a playful camera arc revealing the loop. **Dialogue:** Robo: “I can follow them! Wait... why do they look familiar?” **Sound effects:** Soft mechanical footsteps and a playful electronic wobble.

### Scene 4 — Luna Spots a Pattern (15–20s)

**Scene:** Luna kneels beside the animal tracks and points out two smaller prints behind two larger prints in the direction of travel. Max studies the pattern. **Action & expression:** Luna smiles thoughtfully; Max follows her gesture and looks pleasantly surprised as he understands. **Camera:** Close-up of the tracks, then a medium two-shot; gentle pan along the trail. **Dialogue:** Luna: “Look closely. Two big prints land ahead of two small ones. Something hopped along!” **Sound effects:** Leaf rustle and a soft discovery sparkle.

### Scene 5 — A Little Rabbit Appears (20–25s)

**Scene:** A small brown-and-cream rabbit emerges from a leafy bush beside the trail and makes one gentle hop. Max and Luna watch quietly from a respectful distance. **Action & expression:** Max's eyes widen with delight; Luna smiles warmly; the rabbit remains calm. **Camera:** Reveal from behind the friends, then a low close-up of the rabbit hopping. **Dialogue:** Max: “A rabbit!” Luna: “Its strong back legs help it hop.” **Sound effects:** Rustling leaves, a soft hop, and a warm discovery chime.

### Scene 6 — Mystery Solved! (25–30s)

**Scene:** Robo rejoins the friends, looks from the rabbit to the tracks, and realizes the mystery is solved as the rabbit hops peacefully away along the trail. **Action & expression:** Robo's screen changes to happy crescent eyes; Max gives a small wave and Luna smiles. **Camera:** Medium group shot, gentle pull-back, then a brief pan following the rabbit before settling on the friends. **Dialogue:** Robo: “Case solved! Beep boop!” Max: “Hello, little hopper!” **Sound effects:** Friendly success chime, soft rabbit hops, birdsong, and a cheerful musical finish.

## Continuity Notes

- Keep all six scenes on the same bright forest exploration trail, matching the world-building reference in `docs/world-building/03_forest_exploration_trail.jpg`.
- Preserve the character designs and proportions from the Max, Luna, and Robo character references in `docs/characters`.
- Keep the rabbit's small animal tracks distinct from Robo's larger mechanical bootprints.
- Show the larger hind-foot impressions ahead of the smaller front-foot impressions in the direction of travel.
- The tracks suggest hopping; the rabbit's appearance confirms the discovery.
- The friends observe the rabbit without touching, feeding, chasing, or frightening it.
- Maintain warm morning light, gentle movement, and a playful, child-friendly tone throughout.
