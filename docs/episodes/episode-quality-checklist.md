# Episode quality checklist

## Creative preparation

* [ ] Original story concept selected
* [ ] Target age and educational objective checked
* [ ] Script reviewed for age-appropriate language
* [ ] Narrative has a beginning, middle and ending

## Characters

* [ ] Correct character references loaded
* [ ] Character appearance consistent across scenes
* [ ] Expressions and movements look natural

## Animation

* [ ] All scenes generated successfully
* [ ] No visual distortions or unwanted objects

## Audio

* [ ] Narration is clear and correctly pronounced
* [ ] Music and sound effects are licensed
* [ ] Narration and animation are synchronized

## Editing

* [ ] Vertical 1080 × 1920 export
* [ ] No unintended black frames or clipping
* [ ] Audio levels and subtitles checked

## Safety

* [ ] No frightening, unsafe or misleading content
* [ ] No copyrighted characters or unlicensed assets
* [ ] Educational information verified

## Publishing

* [ ] Correct Made for Kids classification
* [ ] Synthetic-media disclosure evaluated
* [ ] Title and description reviewed
* [ ] Final human approval recorded
